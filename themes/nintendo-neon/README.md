# Nintendo Neon (BloomBox fan theme)

A neon-green animated desktop with **Pixel Time**, a moving Matrix-style wallpaper, and five Nintendo-inspired GIF widgets.

Created by **Adri** and shared as an unofficial fan theme. Nintendo imagery and characters remain the property of their respective rights holders; this is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo.

The theme is saved as 24 small text parts because a full `.bbtheme` is approximately 20 MB. BloomBox Theme Store automatically downloads, joins and verifies every part with SHA-256.

To reconstruct a standalone `.bbtheme` after cloning the catalog, run:

```sh
python3 themes/nintendo-neon/assemble.py
```

This creates `themes/nintendo-neon/Nintendo-Neon.bbtheme`. Do not upload app credentials or personal information inside public themes; this shared version has app-specific identifiers and tap actions removed.
